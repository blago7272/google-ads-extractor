H2 carries the year — so 2026's record H1 sets up, but doesn't guarantee, a record full year (on the historical H1≈45%-of-year pattern, 2026 tracks toward ~€830k, ~+15% vs 2025).
  • Refund rate stepped up from ~4.4% of transactions (2023) to ~6% and has held there since — stable, not deteriorating.

  • Orders with discounts and ad spend move together — their deseasonalized monthly correlation is +0.58, so a raw discount/revenue relationship is confounded by advertising activity.
  • After controlling for Google Ads spend, the discounted-order share has weak correlations with orders (+0.10), gross revenue (+0.21), and gross order value (+0.06). This is not evidence of incremental discount lift.
  • Gross-order-revenue ROAS is sliding: 13.1 → 11.9 → 10.9 → 9.2 per €1 of Google Ads (2023→2026 YTD through Jul 28). It includes shipping.

Caveats: correlation ≠ causation; Meta/organic spend is not included, so the spend control is partial; ROAS is blended, not incremental; the measure counts whether an order has a discount, not its size. Dashboard legend

Scope, data coverage and definitions for the dashboard views.

How to read the charts

  • By year plots Jan–Dec on the x-axis with one line per year, making the same calendar month directly comparable across 2023–2026.
  • Timeline places the same monthly values in one continuous series.
  • Click a year in the chart legend to show or hide it.

Data coverage and currency

  • July and August 2026 sales KPIs are full-month actuals rebuilt from reconciled Selmatic raw tables. September sales KPIs are actual through Sep 13; its completed-order discount share uses the matching Site Admin extract. Google Ads actual spend and ROAS use the same Sep 1–13 window as the September sales actuals.
  • All values are shown in EUR. 2023–25 values recorded in BGN are standardised at the fixed 1.95583 BGN/EUR rate.
  • Monthly grain absorbs the 2023–24 weekend-posting artifact, where weekend orders were Monday-batched; daily and weekday comparisons across years are therefore not reliable.

Sales and merchandise definitions

  • Transaction = one distinct Продажба № single-dated document. A refund is a separate credit-note document with a net value below zero. Refunds are attributed to the month processed because the source does not link a credit note to the original sale.
  • Gross transactions count sale documents only. Net transactions subtract refund documents. Revenue net of refunds equals gross revenue less refunds processed in that month. Revenue retains VAT.
  • Net merchandise revenue includes merchandise only: delivery, vouchers, fully free promotional lines, legacy zero-priced gifts and lines labelled Подарък are excluded. AOV and items per order use the same scope.

Discount and advertising definitions

  • Orders with a discount is the monthly share of completed Website Orders-export records (Status = C) where Discount > 0 OR Subtotal discount > 0. It measures discount prevalence, not discount size.
  • Google Ads spend covers the supplied Google Ads data only. Correlations and blended ROAS are descriptive, not causal or incremental estimates; Meta and organic spend are not included.
  • The daily cost-per-gross-transaction detail covers Jul 1–28 and Aug 1–31, 2026. Its denominator excludes refund documents.

  • Qualified traffic, not impressions, is the useful signal. After controlling for each date and clock hour, clicks and spend remain positively associated with purchases in the following one to two hours.
  • The effect is real but modest. Ads activity adds roughly 1–2 percentage points of explanatory power beyond normal day and hour patterns.
  • Weak days are not explained by traffic alone. Specific low-traffic intervals can constrain purchases, while demand, conversion rate, product availability and channel mix remain important.
